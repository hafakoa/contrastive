# Data Instructions

Datasets are not included.

## Required datasets:
- ReCANVo : A Dataset of Real-World Communicative and Affective Nonverbal Vocalizations
- TalkBank (Dutch) : A Dataset of human communication with an emphasis on spoken communication
- UClass : University College London’s Archive of Stuttered Speech

## Steps:
1. Download datasets from official sources:
  * ReCANVo at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5786860
  * TalkBank at https://talkbank.org
  * UClass at https://www.uclass.psychol.ucl.ac.uk/Audio/wav/
3. Preprocess using:
   python src/utils/preprocess.py
